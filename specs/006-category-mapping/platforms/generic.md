## Primary source checks (2026-10-17)
- [Schema.org category](https://schema.org/category), development vocabulary V30.1 dated 2026-09-16: category accepts Text, URL, Thing and CategoryCode (also PhysicalActivityCategory). Delimiters can indicate informal hierarchy; they do not prove canonical IDs or unambiguous ancestry. Category property version is not merchant taxonomy version.
- [Google category specification](https://support.google.com/merchants/answer/6324436?hl=en): google_product_category accepts ID OR full path, non-repeated. product_type is merchant-defined. Taxonomy evolves; accepted historical values can be translated by Google. Feed value alone supplies no taxonomy release. Do not invent current version or translate without a pinned trusted source/configuration.
- OpenGraph category vocabulary/version and repeat semantics not independently researched within two-page cap; preserve observed raw field without claiming standard semantics.
