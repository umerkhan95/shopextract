One synthetic generic JSON-LD refrigerator HTML smoke first. Expected current
state: parser retains additionalProperty label/value and top-level model, title
observed; normalizer exposes no quantity/model attributes. Portable roundtrip and
real temporary MaintenanceStore checkpoint/reopen retain observed title and
unsupported capacity. Save this smoke before5 native lane expansion.

Then5 native adapters each process1 source-shaped product through their real
extractor with only external HTTP transport controlled via httpx.MockTransport.
Expect common title captured/observed, no supported selected quantity fields.
Include realistic native capacity locations where schema supplies them, but do
not claim those are operational attributes. Portable+Store+engine._supported
checks real paths. No hand-populated Product attributes, no fake parser/store.
At most6products/10 controlled requests,one worker,60s aggregate,16KiB raw/product,
128KiB aggregate/log cap; stop first failure,one diagnosed fixture repair. All
URLs and observations labelled synthetic, no live/network/model activity.

After saved6-lane audit, permit one additional synthetic carrier-only control
(max7products total): explicit raw.attributes/capacity wrapper through existing
capture/normalize/portable/Store/_supported, expected observed wrapper and exact
roundtrip. This demonstrates carrier feasibility only, not SchemaOrg projection
or live extraction availability. Same16KiB product/128KiB aggregate constraints.
