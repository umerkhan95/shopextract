## State and compatibility contracts

- Source record identity and capture are immutable inputs. Normalize/categorize/group/compare produce explicit versioned projections; they cannot mutate Product constructors, scalar defaults, supplier canonical IDs or old snapshots.
- Category classification is `unmapped/unavailable/ambiguous/mapped`; only an explicit unambiguous decision produces one effective Google target. Product support remains native observed/unknown/unsupported/conflicting/expired, never inferred from category existence.
- Candidate→accepted group requires supported native matching evidence plus all-member conflict checks; rejected/ambiguous records remain visible. No transitive merge shortcut. Member-set changes create revisions, not silently reassigned global IDs. Group revision is the pagination snapshot.
- Offer input→validated observation→current-as-of/previous/stale/conflicting→eligible cohort or excluded-with-reason. Replay is idempotent by scoped observation identity, not GTIN alone. Future observations do not enter an earlier as-of report.
- Native specifications and optional normalized projections preserve raw value/owner/policy/evidence. A failed conversion changes projected comparability to unknown while retaining original source data. Same physical dimension is not semantic equivalence.
- Publish stage→validated atomic commit or rollback; cancellation closes owned resources. Local history is not silently overwritten when catalog/unit/matching policy changes. Imported evidence never becomes fresh by loading.
- Initial bounded aggregate public request: maximum1000 source records, up to100 attributes per record, total admitted JSON1MiB, depth64/nodes100000, page size1–100 and response1MiB including envelope/newline. Lower native limits win. Proposed candidate/conflict evaluation budget10,000 and group member cap100 are global deterministic admission bounds, not performance claims. Test just-below/at/above in named boundary mechanism case31; group/constraint checks share the budget and exhaustion leaves the affected component partial/review-only. Canonical input ordering must give identical conservative results. Projection growth that cannot fit fails explicitly or uses declared pages; never truncate. Full official taxonomy is a separate finite resource, not copied into products/requests. Skeleton must reconcile exact encoded counting and error/cursor contracts before implementation; changes return to G1.
